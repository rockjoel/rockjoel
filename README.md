## Before — pre-AI craft

### Early-stage hardware venture (health / mobility device)

Co-founded / contributed to an early product effort around a **physical sensing device** (health-adjacent / mobility). Role on the **hardware & mechanical** side — not the full company story, and **no product, brand, or patent identifiers** published here on purpose.

What that involved in practice:

- **CAD → plastic → metal** — 3D model the part, validate with **3D-printed** plastic, then have a **metal-mechanical shop** produce a durable version
- **Load-cell sensing** — integrate force/load measurement into the assembly; iterate for usable field readings
- **BLE path to a phone** — hardware/firmware side so measurement data could reach a mobile app over Bluetooth; a collaborator owned the phone software
- **Bench reality-check** — as many physical trials as practical; report what worked, what drifted, what still failed

This is capability evidence (design → fab → sense → wireless handoff). It is **not** a claim of certified medical device, clinical validation, or regulatory approval.
