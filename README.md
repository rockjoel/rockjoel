## Before — pre-AI craft

Long stretch of **hardware-first work** with collaborators: datasheets, forums, broken boards, and whatever tools were on the table. Primary lane was physical bring-up — mechanics, sensing, low-voltage, and prototype QA. Companion apps and backends were usually owned by a software collaborator; we still cross-checked hardware choices together. Same buyer label: **prototype QA & guided low-voltage hardware**; slower feedback loops.

- Electromechanical sensing prototype for an early health/mobility product: mechanical design, additive prototypes, metal fabrication with a machine shop, load-cell integration, and a Bluetooth path to a companion app owned by a software collaborator. Extensive bench validation. Not a certified medical device.
- **Low-voltage builds** — plug-in supplies, enclosures, sensor and Wi‑Fi nodes (not licensed mains / electrician work)
- Early **PCB sketches** with Fritzing / Eagle (shared with collaborators where noted in the record)
- **Outdoor sensing & remote monitoring** prototypes (MCU/SBC, env sensors, logging, cellular-era uplinks) — hardware/capability-level only
- **Shop fabrication** — CNC / small-run experiments, not industrial certification

Public notes from that era: **[bench-craft](https://github.com/rockjoel/bench-craft)**.
